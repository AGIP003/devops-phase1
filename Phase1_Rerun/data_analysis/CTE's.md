## CTE Insight
CTEs break complex queries into readable steps.
Think of them as intermediate tables.

## Window Function Insight
Window functions allow calculations without losing row-level detail.

## Growth Insight
Absolute change is misleading.
Growth % normalizes differences.

## Trend Rule
1 month = noise  
3 months = trend  
5+ months = reliable pattern  

## Moving Average Insight
Used to smooth volatility and reveal real direction.

## Outlier Insight
Large transactions distort averages.
Always compare mean vs median or category averages.
